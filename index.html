Our Baking Story

Crafting joy and sourdough, one batch at a time.

From Campus Pop-Up to Neighborhood Hearth

Sweet Bakes started with a simple passion: to bring genuine European-style baking to the student community near GL Bajaj in Greater Noida. What began as a hostel baking experiment quickly expanded into a local favorite.

We believe baking is an art that shouldn't be rushed. That is why our sourdough ferments slowly for 24 hours, and our croissants are laminated with real butter over days to achieve dozens of paper-thin layers.

Supporting our community is core to what we do. We source our flour from local mills and collaborate with campus clubs for pop-up workshops.

100% Honest Ingredients

Real farm butter, locally milled grain flour, and fresh seasonal fruits. Absolutely zero preservatives or margarine.

Student-Friendly Pricing

Special combi deals and a flat 15% discount for students to make high-quality artisan pastries accessible.

Baking Workshops

From basic bread shaping to advanced pastry lamination, we run weekly hands-on baking classes for beginners.
